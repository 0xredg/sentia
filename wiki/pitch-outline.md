# Pitch Outline

## Goal

Deliver a clear, memorable pitch in under 3 minutes that makes Sentia feel native to World and easy to understand.

## Suggested Structure

### 0:00-0:30 — Team

Introduce the team in one sentence each.

Template:

"We are building Sentia, the human intelligence layer of the internet. We are a team focused on making verified human judgment available on demand."

### 0:30-1:10 — Idea

Explain the problem and product simply.

Template:

"AI is powerful, but it still fails on nuance, ambiguity, and trust. Sentia lets products call verified humans in real time for tasks AI cannot reliably solve."

### 1:10-1:30 — Why World

Show why the product belongs on World specifically.

Template:

"Sentia works especially well on World because World ID proves each worker is a unique human, and World wallet infrastructure enables instant rewards inside a mobile-native app."

### 1:30-3:00 — Demo

Show the shortest possible complete loop:

1. a task is created
2. a verified human answers it
3. the answer is returned
4. the reward is paid

## Demo Script Direction

During the product demo, reinforce these points:

- this responder is a verified human
- this is a task where AI struggles
- the response arrives quickly
- the reward happens immediately

## Best Example Line

"When AI hits ambiguity, Sentia routes the task to a real human and returns a trusted answer in seconds."

## Closing

End with a compact vision statement:

"We believe World can become the distribution layer for human intelligence, and Sentia is the product that makes that usable for apps, AI systems, and platforms."

## Recording Notes

- use a mobile-sized product view
- avoid long setup sequences
- remove dead time and loading delays where possible
- keep the narration energetic and simple
- make sure the UI text is legible in a recorded video

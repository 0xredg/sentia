# Project Vision

## Sentia

Sentia is building the human intelligence layer of the internet.

In a world increasingly dominated by AI and bots, Sentia ensures that real human input remains accessible, reliable, and instantly usable.

## One-liner

Sentia connects real, verified humans to tasks that AI can't solve and pays them instantly.

## Core Idea

Sentia enables companies and applications to access verified human thinking on demand for tasks that AI cannot reliably solve.

Every action on Sentia is:

- performed by a real, unique human
- instantly rewarded via crypto payments
- usable in real time by products, AI systems, or platforms

## Problem

Today:

- AI still struggles with nuance, judgment, ambiguity, and edge cases
- many platforms are flooded with bots, fake users, and low-trust interactions
- human-powered services are often slow, fragmented, and expensive

This creates a gap in the internet stack: products need trustworthy human input, but existing systems are not fast, global, or programmable enough.

## Why Now

New infrastructure makes Sentia possible:

- proof-of-human systems such as Worldcoin make it possible to verify that someone is human and unique at global scale
- blockchains make instant, programmatic, borderless payments possible

Together, these unlock a new primitive: a real-time, trust-minimized market for human intelligence.

## What Sentia Becomes

Sentia is not just a task app.

It becomes a human API for the internet.

Any product should be able to:

- request human judgment
- get answers in seconds
- trust that responses come from real people

## Example Use Cases

- AI companies collecting clean, bot-free training data
- apps outsourcing sensitive content moderation to real humans
- startups getting instant qualitative feedback from users
- protocols running sybil-resistant voting or governance actions

## Product Principles

- Human first: every response must come from a verified real person
- Fast by default: human input should arrive in seconds, not hours
- Programmable: products and agents should be able to request human work via API-like flows
- Fair incentives: contributors should be rewarded instantly and transparently
- Trustworthy outputs: the system should maximize quality, uniqueness, and resistance to spam

## Hackathon Framing

For the hackathon, Sentia should be presented as the infrastructure layer that lets apps and AI systems call real humans when machine intelligence is not enough.

The demo should make three things obvious:

- the worker is a real verified human
- the task is something AI alone cannot reliably solve
- the payment and result delivery happen fast enough to feel like an API

## Positioning

Sentia sits at the intersection of:

- AI infrastructure
- proof of personhood
- crypto payments
- human-in-the-loop systems

## Short Pitch

Sentia gives the internet access to real human judgment on demand.

When AI fails on nuance, trust, or ambiguity, Sentia routes the task to verified humans, returns answers in real time, and pays contributors instantly.

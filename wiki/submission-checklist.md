# Submission Checklist

## Non-Negotiables

- pitch is in English
- video is under 3 minutes
- video link is unlisted YouTube or Loom only
- submission is sent before Sunday, April 26, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET
- repo is public and open source
- repo includes a valid open-source license file
- prototype is functional or clearly supported by visible code
- no commits are pushed after the official deadline

## Recommended Repo Checklist

- public GitHub repository
- `README.md` with product summary and setup instructions
- `LICENSE` file using MIT or another permissive license
- working Mini App or clear demoable prototype
- clear note on how World ID, wallet, and optionally World Chat are used
- screenshots or demo assets if useful

## Product Checklist

- app works on mobile-sized screens
- core user flow is understandable without explanation
- World-native features are integrated for real product reasons
- at least one moment clearly demonstrates verified humanity
- payout or reward logic is visible in the product story

## Pitch Checklist

- introduce team and roles
- explain the problem simply
- explain the product in one sentence
- explain why this belongs on World
- show the product working
- end with the vision and why the project can keep growing after the hackathon

## Judge Optimization Checklist

- World stack usage feels essential, not cosmetic
- the app is understandable by non-crypto users
- the UX is clean and fast
- the value proposition is obvious in under 30 seconds
- the demo has one memorable moment

## Timing Checklist

- final code freeze before the deadline
- record the demo with enough buffer for upload issues
- verify the submitted video link opens without permission issues
- verify no late commits land after deadline
- submit before the last hour if possible

## Seoul And Program Readiness

- confirm availability for Seoul from May 10-18, 2026
- confirm ability to present in English
- confirm travel readiness if selected
- prepare a short explanation of why the team can keep building after the hackathon

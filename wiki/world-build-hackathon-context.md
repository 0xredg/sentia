# World Build Hackathon Context

## Program Overview

World Build Labs positions World Build as an elite builder program for founders building on the World ecosystem.

Key points from the program pack:

- 5-month acceleration program
- built around World Mini Apps inside World App
- targeted at founders building directly on World
- World is presented as a network of 38M+ users across 160 countries
- World Build founders reportedly reached 6M users and raised $15M+ in the last 12 months

## Why This Matters For Sentia

The program strongly favors products that feel native to World rather than generic crypto apps ported over.

For Sentia, this means the project should clearly show:

- proof of unique humanity via World ID
- mobile-first UX inside World App
- wallet-native payout flows
- optionally World Chat for engagement, task notifications, or coordination

## Program Stages

1. Stage 1: Apply and Hack
   April 23-26, 2026

   Q3 2026 in San Francisco

## What World Wants Builders To Build

The pack emphasizes:

- Mini Apps built specifically for World
- products for a 100% mobile audience
- UX that works for non-crypto-native users
- use of World ID when uniqueness, fairness, or human-only access matters
- use of wallet and transaction primitives inside World App
- use of World Chat when communication and retention matter

Product angles they appear to like:

- one-human-one-account experiences
- fair access products
- bot-resistant community features
- products that drive utility, engagement, or DAUs in World
- apps that reach new audiences
- fun and creative apps, not only serious infra products

## Judging Criteria

Applications are scored out of 10 on:

1. Use of World Stack
   They care about meaningful use of World wallet, World ID, World Chat, and the global user base.
2. Idea Quality
   They care about value proposition, viability, creativity, impact, and whether it expands the World ecosystem.
3. Build Quality
   They care about demo quality, product clarity, UX/UI, and whether the app works.
4. Team
   They care about execution ability, product skill, Web3 understanding, and presentation.
5. Wildcard
   Judges may reward something especially cool, memorable, or unique.

## Practical Interpretation

The clearest judging signals are:

- use World-native primitives in a way that feels necessary, not forced
- show a clear and useful product that is easy to understand
- deliver a clean 3-minute demo
- explain why the product makes sense specifically on World
- make the app understandable for mainstream mobile users

## Submission Format

- judging is asynchronous via video pitch
- accepted links: unlisted YouTube or Loom only
- maximum pitch length: under 3 minutes
- required fields include team name, email, Telegram handle, and video link
- presentations must be in English

Recommended pitch structure from the guide:

- team intro: 30 seconds
- idea: 40 seconds
- why World: 20 seconds
- demo: 90 seconds

## Hard Requirements And Constraints

- final submission must be open source and publicly reviewable
- include a functional prototype or strong proof of work in progress
- include a valid open-source license, ideally MIT or similarly permissive
- code must be uploaded to the World repo or linked from a public personal repo
- commits timestamped after the deadline are disqualifying
- maximum team size is 6
- only 2 Seoul spots are offered per selected team
- participants must be 18+
- winners may need to pass KYC/AML to receive prizes

## Strategic Takeaway For Sentia

Sentia is a strong fit if it is framed as a World-native human layer:

- World ID proves each worker is a unique human
- World wallet enables instant rewards
- World Chat can distribute tasks, updates, or re-engagement
- the Mini App makes human intelligence feel like a mobile-native API
